How We Write

Every Story. Three Layers.

Here's a real one, broken down the MBA India Daily way:

Sample · RBI Monetary Policy
1 Layer 1 — What Happened

RBI cuts the repo rate by 25 bps to 6.25%.

The first cut in nearly five years. Most headlines stop here.

2 Layer 2 — Why It Matters

Cheaper credit ripples through autos, housing and bank margins.

EMIs fall, loan growth accelerates, deposit rates follow downward — savers lose, borrowers win, and rate-sensitive sectors re-price within weeks.

3 Layer 3 — What a Manager Should Do

Act before the market finishes re-pricing.

Selling EMI-driven products? Re-run your affordability messaging this week. Managing cash? Lock today's FD rates before they slide. That's the difference between reading news and reading reality.
